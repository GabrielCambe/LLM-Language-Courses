# The study system

A routine built to survive bad days. The rule is: **never skip the minimum.
Everything above the minimum is a bonus.**

---

## The three session sizes

### 🟢 Minimum viable day (5 min): counts as a full "yes, I studied"
- Open the app → **Flashcards** → clear the due cards (or 5 minutes, whichever comes first).

That's it. On bad days, this keeps the spaced-repetition chain alive, and that chain matters
more than any single long session.

### 🟡 Normal day (25 min, one focus block)

| Min | Activity | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Flashcards (due cards) | Spaced retrieval |
| 5–12 | **Rule of the unit**: read the rule *once*, then do the drill in the app | Explicit instruction → practice |
| 12–20 | **Media**: this unit's song or clip, with the Hunt task | Input + noticing |
| 20–25 | **Output**: 3–5 sentences written or *spoken aloud* to the LLM tutor, get corrected | Output + feedback |

### 🔵 Deep day (45–60 min, weekends or high-energy days)
Normal day, plus one of:
- a full episode / film segment with French subtitles (re-watch one scene twice),
- a 10-minute **conversation** with the LLM (voice mode if possible),
- **shadowing** a song chorus or podcast clip 5×,
- Duolingo, *only if you enjoy it*, as dessert.

---

## The weekly shape

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Mon–Thu | Normal days on the **current unit** |
| Fri | **Review day**: interleaved drill (all tenses/rules so far, mixed) + redo the unit's checklist |
| Sat | Deep day: media binge (a film, an album with lyrics, a series episode) |
| Sun | Rest, or minimum viable day. Write one line in [`french/progress-log.md`](../french/progress-log.md). |

**One unit ≈ one week.** Some units take two weeks. That's fine. Move on when the unit's
**checklist** is done, not when the calendar says so.

## Implementation intentions (set yours now)

Research on goal pursuit (Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006) shows that "**When X, I will Y**"
plans work much better than "I'll study French more". Fill these in:

- When I ______________ (e.g. *pour my first coffee*), I will **open the flashcards**.
- When I ______________ (e.g. *sit down after lunch*), I will **do the 25-min block**.
- When I'm on my commute / at the gym / washing dishes, I will **listen to this unit's playlist**.

## 🧭 Scope guard (read this when you feel the urge to "improve the system")

You build AI agents for a living, so the urge to build *a better course* instead of *taking
the course* will be strong. That's procrastination that feels productive. Some guardrails:

1. **The parking lot.** Got an idea mid-session ("I should make the app do X!", "let me
   research the best SRS algorithm")? Write it in the **Parking Lot** box in the app (or
   [`parking-lot.md`](parking-lot.md)) and keep studying. Review the lot once a week.
2. **Building budget.** At most **1 hour of building for every 5 hours of studying**.
   The progress log keeps you honest.
3. **"Is this learning French or learning *about* learning French?"** Both are fine, but
   only the first one counts toward the 600 hours.
4. **Content before tools.** A plain Markdown unit you actually finish beats a perfect app
   you never use.

---

## When you fall off (you will; everyone does)

- No guilt, no "starting over". Open the app; the SRS has kept your place.
- If you've been away more than a week, **do flashcards only** for 3 days before new content.
- If a unit feels boring, skip ahead to its media section. Interest is a resource; use it.
