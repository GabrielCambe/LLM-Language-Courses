# Unit 3: Having and describing (avoir + adjectives) · CEFR A1 · ~7 days

## 🎯 Can-do goals
- I can say my age, and that I'm hungry, cold, afraid, or right.
- I can describe people and things with adjectives that agree.
- I can hear the difference between *ils sont* and *ils ont*.

## 📐 The rule

### Avoir (to have): irregular

| | |
|---|---|
| j'**ai** | (elision: *je → j'*) |
| tu **as** | |
| il / elle / on **a** | (*a* without accent = has; *à* = to) |
| nous **avons** | "noo-**z**a-vohn" |
| vous **avez** | "voo-**z**a-vay" |
| ils / elles **ont** | "eel-**z**ohn" ⚠️ |

**The crucial pair:** *ils **s**ont* ("eel **s**ohn", they are) vs *ils **ont*** ("eel **z**ohn", they have).
The only difference is **s vs z**.

### Expressions with avoir (where English uses "to be")

| French | English |
|---|---|
| avoir **30 ans** | to be 30 years old |
| avoir **faim / soif** | to be hungry / thirsty |
| avoir **chaud / froid** | to be hot / cold |
| avoir **peur** (de) | to be afraid (of) |
| avoir **raison / tort** | to be right / wrong |
| avoir **besoin de** | to need |
| avoir **envie de** | to feel like / want |
| avoir **la flemme** (de) | to not be bothered (to), to feel lazy · *colloquial* |

### Adjective agreement
Adjectives agree in **gender and number** with the noun:

| | masculine | feminine |
|---|---|---|
| singular | petit | petit**e** |
| plural | petit**s** | petit**es** |

🔊 **Pronunciation bonus:** adding *-e* makes the silent final consonant **audible**:
*petit* "puh-tee" → *petite* "puh-teet"; *grand* "grahn" → *grande* "grahnd". That's how you *hear* the feminine.

Adjectives already ending in **-e** don't change in the feminine: *facile, jeune, belge, rouge*.

**Common irregular feminines**

| m. | f. | pattern |
|---|---|---|
| heureux | heureuse | -eux → -euse |
| actif | active | -f → -ve |
| bon | bonne | double consonant |
| italien | italienne | double consonant |
| blanc | blanche | |
| beau / nouveau / vieux | belle / nouvelle / vieille | fully irregular, learn as chunks |

### Placement: most adjectives go **after** the noun
*une voiture **rouge**, un film **intéressant**.*
**BAGS** adjectives go **before**: **B**eauty (*beau, joli*), **A**ge (*jeune, vieux, nouveau*),
**G**ood/bad (*bon, mauvais*), **S**ize (*petit, grand, gros*): *une **petite** maison, un **bon** café.*

### 📍 Where it goes in the sentence
**determiner + (BAGS) + noun + (other adjectives)**: *une **petite** voiture **rouge***.
See section 2 of the [sentence map](../grammar/09-sentence-structure.md).

## ⚠️ Common traps
- ❌ *Je suis 30 ans.* → ✅ *J'**ai** 30 ans.*
- ❌ *Je suis froid.* (= "I'm a cold person" 🥶) → ✅ *J'**ai** froid.*
- ❌ *une maison petite* → ✅ *une petite maison* (BAGS)

## 🧱 Vocabulary (Unit 3 deck)
*avoir + all expressions above, grand(e), petit(e), beau/belle, nouveau/nouvelle, vieux/vieille,
bon(ne), mauvais(e), content(e), fatigué(e), heureux/heureuse, facile, difficile, intéressant(e), jeune.*

## 🎧 Input + Hunt
**Angèle: *Flemme*** (album *Brol*, 2018). The title comes from ***avoir la flemme***.
- **Hunt:** find every form of **avoir** in the lyrics (*j'ai, t'as, a, ont...*). Note: ***t'as*** = *tu as*
  in spoken French; the vowel of *tu* gets elided in casual speech, though never in formal writing.
- **Hunt 2:** find adjectives and check their agreement.

## 🗣️ Use it
- **Write:** describe 3 people you know (age, personality, appearance) with *avoir* + adjectives.
- **Speak:** ask the Conversation Partner to play a friend, and tell them how you feel today
  (*J'ai faim, j'ai la flemme de travailler...*).

## 💡 Curiosity
***Brol***, the name of Angèle's first album, is **Belgian French** for "mess, stuff, junk"
(*Range ton brol !* = Tidy up your stuff!). It comes from Brussels dialect, and most people in
France wouldn't use it. Belgian French has many of these: ***une fois*** as a filler, ***le GSM***
(mobile phone), ***à tantôt*** (see you later).
