# Unit 4: Doing things (-ER verbs) · CEFR A1 · ~7–10 days

## 🎯 Can-do goals
- I can conjugate any regular -ER verb in the present (about 90% of French verbs!).
- I can talk about what I like, where I live and what I do.
- I can say how often I do things.

## 📐 The rule

### Regular -ER verbs: remove *-er*, add the ending
**parler** (to speak) → stem **parl-**

| | ending | form | sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| je | -e | parl**e** | "parl" |
| tu | -es | parl**es** | "parl" |
| il/elle/on | -e | parl**e** | "parl" |
| nous | -ons | parl**ons** | "par-lohn" |
| vous | -ez | parl**ez** | "par-lay" |
| ils/elles | -ent | parl**ent** | "parl" ⚠️ silent |

🎉 **Four of the six forms sound identical.** In speech you only need to learn 3 sounds: *parl*, *parlons*, *parlez*.

With a vowel or mute h at the start: **j'**aime, **j'**habite, **j'**écoute, with liaison
*nous_aimons, ils_habitent* ("eel-za-beet").

### Present = 3 English tenses
*Je travaille* = I work / I am working / I do work. There's no separate "-ing" form.
To stress "right now", use ***être en train de*** + infinitive: *Je suis en train de travailler.*

### Spelling tweaks (pronunciation stays regular)
- **-ger:** *nous mang**e**ons* (keeps the soft g)
- **-cer:** *nous commen**ç**ons* (keeps the soft c)
- **préférer:** *je préf**è**re* (é → è when the ending is silent)

### Verb + infinitive
*J'aime **chanter**. Je déteste **attendre**. J'adore **danser**.*
And remember Unit 2: *J'aime **la** musique.* (general category = definite article).

### Frequency adverbs (go **after** the verb)
*toujours* (always), *souvent* (often), *parfois* (sometimes), *rarement* (rarely):
*Je travaille **souvent** le soir.*

### 📍 Where it goes in the sentence
**Subject + verb + adverb + rest**: *Je travaille **souvent** le soir.* The adverb never goes between subject and verb.
See sections 1 and 4 of the [sentence map](../grammar/09-sentence-structure.md).

## ⚠️ Common traps
- Pronouncing *-ent*: ❌ "ils parl-ENT" → ✅ "eel parl".
- ❌ *Je suis travaille* / *Je suis travailler* (copying "I am working") → ✅ *Je travaille.*
- ❌ *Je souvent travaille.* → ✅ *Je travaille souvent.*

## 💡 Curiosity
New French verbs are almost always created as **-ER verbs**, so the class keeps growing:
*liker* (to like on social media), *googler*, *spammer*, *scroller*, *checker*. The Académie
française frowns on many of them. Québec, meanwhile, invented its own words instead of borrowing:
***clavarder*** (to chat online) = ***clavier*** (keyboard) + ***bavarder*** (to chat).

Also: **Roméo Elvis is Angèle's older brother.**
