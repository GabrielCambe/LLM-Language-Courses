# Unit 2: Things and people (gender & articles) · CEFR A1 · ~7 days

## 🎯 Can-do goals
- I can say what there is in a place (*il y a...*).
- I can use *un/une/des* and *le/la/les* correctly.
- I can guess the gender of many nouns from their ending.

## 📐 The rule

### Every noun has a gender: masculine or feminine
There's usually no logic to it (a *table* is feminine, a *book* is masculine), so **always learn
nouns with their article**: not "*maison*" but "***la** maison*". The flashcards always show the article.

### Articles

| | masculine | feminine | plural (both) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **definite** (the) | **le** | **la** | **les** |
| before vowel / mute h | **l'** | **l'** | **les** (+ liaison: *les_amis*) |
| **indefinite** (a/some) | **un** | **une** | **des** |

- ⚠️ ***l'*** hides the gender (*l'eau* is f., *l'ami* is m.), so learn those extra carefully.
- **French uses *le/la/les* for general categories** where English uses nothing:
  *J'aime **la** musique.* (I like music.) *Les chats sont mignons.* (Cats are cute.)
- **des** is required where English says nothing or "some": *Il y a **des** livres.* (There are books.)

### Plural
- Add **-s** (silent!): *le livre → les livres*. You hear the plural in the **article**, not the noun.
- **-eau → -eaux**: *le bateau → les bateaux*. **-al → -aux**: *le journal → les journaux*.
- Already ending in -s/-x/-z: no change (*le pays → les pays*).

### Ending rules for gender (≈ 80% reliable)

| Usually **masculine** | Usually **feminine** |
|---|---|
| **-age** (*le fromage, le voyage*) | **-tion, -sion** (*la nation, la télévision*) |
| **-ment** (*le moment, le gouvernement*) | **-té** (*la liberté, la société*) |
| **-eau** (*le bureau, le gâteau*) | **-ette** (*la baguette, la cigarette*) |
| **-isme** (*le tourisme*) | **-ure** (*la voiture, la culture*) |
| **-oir** (*le soir, le miroir*) | **-ence, -ance** (*la chance, la science*) |
| **-et, -al, -ail** (*le jouet, le journal, le travail*) | **-ie, -ade, -esse, -ude** (*la vie, la promenade*) |

Famous exceptions: *la page, la plage, l'image (f), la cage* · *l'eau (f), la peau* · *le silence* · *le musée, le lycée*.

### *Il y a*: there is / there are
*Il y a un café dans la rue. Il y a des gens.* Invariable: same for singular and plural.

### 📍 Where it goes in the sentence
The main noun comes **first** and details follow: *la chanson **d'**Angèle* (Angèle's song), *une tasse **à** café* (a coffee cup).
See section 2 of the [sentence map](../grammar/09-sentence-structure.md).

## ⚠️ Common traps
- ❌ *J'aime musique.* → ✅ *J'aime **la** musique.*
- ❌ *Il y a livres.* → ✅ *Il y a **des** livres.*
- Gender in French ≠ gender in other languages: *la mer* (f) but Portuguese *o mar* (m); *le lait* (m) but *o leite*... and Spanish *la leche* (f).

## 💡 Curiosity
Some nouns **change meaning with gender**: *le livre* (book) / *la livre* (pound) · *le tour*
(tour, turn) / *la tour* (tower, like *la tour Eiffel*) · *le poste* (job, TV set) / *la poste*
(post office) · *le mode* (method) / *la mode* (fashion).
