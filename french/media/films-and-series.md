# 🎬 Films, series & animation

## How to watch (research-based)
1. **Pick something slightly too hard**, but not *much* too hard. If you understand less than
   ~50% even with subtitles, drop down a level.
2. **Subtitles:**
   - A1: French audio + subtitles in your language, *or* French subtitles if it's a show for children.
   - **A2+: French audio + French subtitles.** This is where vocabulary gains are biggest.
3. **Re-watch** a 3–5 minute scene you liked: first for meaning, then with pause + Scene Explainer prompt.
4. **Language Reactor** (browser extension for Netflix/YouTube) shows dual subtitles and lets
   you click words. Great, but don't spend more time on the tool than on watching.
5. **Short and frequent beats long and rare.** One 20-min episode on 3 days > a film once a week.

## A1: Animation & children's shows (simple language, visual context)

| Title | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Peppa Pig** (in French) | kids' series | Short, slow, everyday vocabulary. Embarrassingly effective. |
| **Trotro** | kids' series | Very simple sentences, 2–3 min episodes |
| **Miraculous: Les aventures de Ladybug et Chat Noir** | animated series | Made in France, set in Paris |
| ***Ernest et Célestine*** (2012) | animated film | Gentle French–Belgian film, clear dialogue |
| ***Le Petit Prince*** (2015) | animated film | Watch the French version; then read the book at A2/B1 |
| ***Ma vie de Courgette*** (2016) | stop-motion film | Swiss-French, kids' dialogue, moving story |
| ***Les Triplettes de Belleville*** (2003) | animated film | Almost no dialogue: pure culture + atmosphere |
| ***Astérix*** films | animated films | Puns galore; watch for fun |
| **Extr@ French** | learners' sitcom (YouTube) | Made for learners: slow and very repetitive |

## Where to find French content
- **TV5Monde** ([apprendre.tv5monde.com](https://apprendre.tv5monde.com)): free videos with exercises, sorted by CEFR level.
- **Netflix**: set the profile language to French; many shows offer French audio + subtitles.
- **YouTube**: search the title + "VF" (*version française*) for dubbed versions.
