# 🎵 Music guide

You already do the most useful thing: **listening while reading the lyrics**. This guide adds
structure so that listening turns into learning.

## The 6-step song study method (≈ 20 min per song, spread over a week)

| Step | What | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Vibe** | Listen once with no lyrics. Just enjoy it and catch any words you can. | Motivation; baseline |
| 2. **Read along** | Listen with French lyrics (YouTube Music shows them). | Captions effect |
| 3. **Decode** | Paste 4–6 lines at a time into the **Song Explainer** prompt. Add 3–5 chunks to your deck (App → Song Workshop → click a word). | Explicit + noticing |
| 4. **Hunt** | Do the unit's Hunt task (find all negations, all *avoir*, etc.). | Noticing |
| 5. **Cloze** | App → Song Workshop → Cloze mode: fill the gaps while listening. | Retrieval practice |
| 6. **Sing / shadow** | Sing the chorus along with her. Then try it without the lyrics. | Singing effect, shadowing |

Repeat steps 5–6 on different days (that's spacing). **Don't do all six steps in one sitting.**

⚠️ **Songs bend grammar for rhythm**, so use them to train your ear and memorise chunks, but
check rules against the grammar reference.

## 🇧🇪 Angèle: your main artist

**Who:** Angèle Van Laeken, singer from Brussels, Belgium (born 1995). Her brother is the rapper
**Roméo Elvis**. Her style is witty, ironic pop with clear diction, great for learners.

**Albums**
| Album | Year | Notes for learners |
|---|---|---|
| ***Brol*** | 2018 | *Brol* = Belgian slang for "stuff/mess". Lots of everyday vocabulary and irony. |
| ***Brol La Suite*** | 2019 | Reissue with extra songs (*Oui ou non*, *Perdus*, *Insomnies*...). |
| ***Nonante-Cinq*** | 2021 | *Nonante-cinq* = 95 in Belgian French (her birth year). |
| ***Nonante-Cinq La Suite*** | 2022 | Reissue, incl. *Évidemment* (with Orelsan). |
| ***Instinct*** | Oct 2026 | Brand new! More electronic (Justice, Soulwax). Save it for A2+. |

**Song → unit map** (✱ = easiest for beginners)
| Song | Album | Use it for | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Bruxelles je t'aime* ✱ | Nonante-Cinq | Elision (*je t'aime*), *être*, Belgian identity | 0, 1 |
| *La Loi de Murphy* | Brol | Nouns + articles, everyday vocabulary | 2 |
| *Flemme* ✱ | Brol | *avoir la flemme*, *avoir* forms, spoken *t'as* | 3 |
| *Tout oublier* (feat. Roméo Elvis) | Brol | -ER verbs (*oublier*), infinitives | 4 |
| *Oui ou non* | Brol La Suite | Questions, yes/no | 5 |
| *Ta reine* ✱ | Brol | Possessives (*ta, ton, tes*) | 6 |
| *Je veux tes yeux* | Brol | *vouloir*, possessives, *les yeux* liaison | 6, 9 |
| *Balance ton quoi* | Brol | Imperative (*balance !*); a feminist song referencing the #BalanceTonPorc movement (France's #MeToo) | 11 |
| *Victime des réseaux* | Brol | Social media vocabulary | A2 |
| *Les matins* | Brol | Daily routine (pronominal verbs) | 16 |
| *Démons* (feat. Damso) | Nonante-Cinq | Mixed sung/rap; listening challenge | A2+ |

> 💡 If you can't find a grammar point in a song, that's fine. Note what you *do* find. The
> goal is active listening, not an exact match.

## Other artists, by difficulty

### ✱ Beginner-friendly (slow, clear, repetitive)
| Artist · Song | Why |
|---|---|
| **Stromae** 🇧🇪 · *Alors on danse* | Very repetitive; the pronoun *on* |
| **Stromae** · *Papaoutai* | Questions in casual style (*où t'es ?*) |
| **Stromae** · *Formidable* | Imparfait (*tu étais...*), adjectives |
| **Zaz** · *Je veux* | *vouloir*, negation, imperative |
| **Édith Piaf** · *Non, je ne regrette rien* | Negation; very clear old-school diction |
| **Édith Piaf** · *La vie en rose* | Object pronouns (*il me dit...*) |
| **Christophe Maé** · *Il est où le bonheur* | Spoken question form (word order) |
| **Louane** · *Jour 1* | Simple vocabulary, numbers/days |
| **Indila** · *Dernière danse* | Slow, emotional, clear vowels |

### Intermediate (A2–B1)
**Clara Luciani** (*La grenade*), **Vianney** (*Je m'en vais*), **Pomme**, **Christine and the Queens**,
**Stromae**'s *Multitude* album (2022), **Juliette Armanet**, **Aya Nakamura** (lots of slang; fun but hard).

### Advanced (fast rap, heavy slang)
**Roméo Elvis** 🇧🇪, **Damso** 🇧🇪, **Orelsan**, **PNL**, **Lomepal**. Try them at B1 for slang and verlan.
