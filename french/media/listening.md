# 🎙️ Podcasts & YouTube (comprehensible input)

Sorted from easiest to hardest. Pick **one** for the current month. Don't subscribe to all of
them (scope guard!).

| Level | Resource | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Coffee Break French** | podcast | Structured lessons in English + French |
| A1–A2 | **Alice Ayel** | YouTube | Comprehensible input: she explains in simple French with drawings |
| A2 | **Easy French** | YouTube | Street interviews with dual subtitles, real spoken French |
| A2 | **Duolingo French Podcast** | podcast | True stories in simple French with English narration |
| A2–B1 | **Français avec Pierre** | YouTube/podcast | Grammar explained *in French* |
| A2–B1 | **Journal en français facile** (RFI) | podcast | 10-min daily news in clear, slow French, with transcripts |
| B1 | **InnerFrench** (Hugo) | podcast/YouTube | Interesting topics at a slowed native pace |
| B1+ | **1jour1actu** | video | News explained for kids, surprisingly good for adults |
| B2 | **France Culture**, **France Inter** | radio/podcasts | Native-level: your long-term goal |

## How to use a podcast episode
1. Listen once while doing something else (passive).
2. Listen again focused, with the transcript if there is one (active).
3. Pick 2 sentences and **shadow** them 5× (repeat in sync with the speaker).
4. Optional: tell the LLM Conversation Partner what the episode was about, in French.
