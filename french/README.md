# French course: roadmap

**Goal:** go from zero to a solid **A2** (survive daily life, follow simple conversations and
songs, talk about past and future) in about **6 months** at ~25 min/day, then continue to B1.

Each unit = **one rule family + ~20 high-frequency words + one piece of media + one output task**.
Units marked ✅ are fully written. The others are on the roadmap and get generated with
[`../docs/unit-template.md`](../docs/unit-template.md) when you reach them, so you study
material that matches your actual progress (that's the LLM experiment).

> 🧭 **Scope guard:** you only need to look at the unit you're on and the next one.
> Ignore the rest of this page until you get there.

## After A2 → B1
- Switch most input to **native media** with French subtitles (see [`media/films-and-series.md`](media/films-and-series.md)).
- Read your first book: *Le Petit Prince* (Saint-Exupéry), then graded readers.
- Weekly real conversation: a tandem partner (Tandem / HelloTalk apps) or a tutor (italki).
