# Japanese course: roadmap

**Goal:** reach a solid **A2** (roughly JLPT N4) in about **10 months** at ~25 min/day: introduce
yourself, shop, get around, talk about your day, past and plans in polite Japanese, and read
kana plus your first ~150 kanji.

Japanese is the longest road in this repo. The US Foreign Service Institute puts it in its hardest
group for English speakers. The grammar is actually quite regular. The time goes into a new writing
system and vocabulary that shares almost nothing with English or Portuguese. So the roadmap starts
with sounds and kana, and adds kanji a few at a time from unit 4 instead of all at once.

Each unit = **one rule family + ~15 high-frequency words + one output task**.

> 🌱 **Status: starter.** The study app has decks for units 0–3 and the Tutor knows the course.

> 🧭 **Scope guard:** you only need the unit you're on and the next one. Kanji, keigo (honorific
> speech) and pitch accent are rabbit holes: the roadmap brings in what you need, when you need it.

|---|---|---|
| 0 | Sounds and hiragana | 5 vowels, the mora "beat", long vowels, small っ (pause), ゛ (dakuten), ゃゅょ |
| 1 | Introductions: *は* and *です* | *わたしは…です*, *はじめまして*, *…さん*, dropping "I" |

| 6 | Particles of action | *を* (object), *に / へ* (to, at a time), *で* (place of action, by means of) |

| 8 | There is: *あります / います* | Things vs living beings, location words (*うえ, なか, となり*) |
| 9 | Past tense | *ました / ませんでした*, *でした*, adjectives in the past |
| 10 | Want and invite | *…たい*, *…ませんか*, *…ましょう* |

## Notes
- ⌨️ **Typing Japanese:** for EN → JA flashcards, add a Japanese keyboard (on a phone: the kana or
  romaji keyboard; on a computer: the system's Japanese input). You type *neko* and it turns into
  ねこ. Typing is optional: you can also say the answer in your head and press *Show*.
- 🔤 **Romaji (Latin letters) is a crutch:** the deck shows it only in units 0–1. Reading kana from
  day one is faster in the long run.
- 🈷️ **Kanji:** each kanji usually has two kinds of readings: a Japanese one (*kun*, e.g. 日 = ひ)
  and one borrowed from Chinese (*on*, e.g. 日 = にち). Learn kanji **inside words**, not as lone
  characters with a list of readings.
- 🎵 **Pitch accent:** Japanese uses pitch (high/low) instead of stress (*はし* can be "chopsticks"
  or "bridge"). Shadowing native audio picks most of it up. Don't study it as a separate topic at A1.
- 🇧🇷 **A Portuguese connection:** Portuguese traders and missionaries reached Japan in the 1500s,
  and a few words stayed: *パン* (pão), *カステラ* (pão de Castela), *ボタン* (botão).
  Brazil also has the largest Japanese community outside Japan.
- **Resources:** the textbooks *Genki I–II* (A1–A2) as the main spine; Tofugu's free kana guide for
  unit 0; *Comprehensible Japanese* (YouTube) and the *Nihongo con Teppei for beginners* podcast for
  easy listening; Studio Ghibli films with Japanese subtitles once kana feels comfortable.
