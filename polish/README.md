# Polish course: roadmap

**Goal:** reach a solid **A2** (introduce yourself, get around, shop, talk about past and future)
in about **8 months** at ~25 min/day. Polish takes longer than French for a Portuguese or English
speaker: 7 cases and verb aspect are new ideas, so the roadmap gives them room.

Each unit = **one rule family + ~15 high-frequency words + one output task**.

> 🌱 **Status: starter.** ❌ **No lesson pages yet: this roadmap is all there is.** The study app has
> decks for units 0–3 and the Tutor knows the course. Generate each lesson page with
> [`../docs/unit-template.md`](../docs/unit-template.md) when you reach it, like the French units.

> 🧭 **Scope guard:** you only need the unit you're on and the next one.

---

| # | Unit | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Sounds and spelling | *sz cz rz/ż*, soft *ś ć ź ń*, nasal *ą ę*, *ł* = w, final consonants lose voice |
| 1 | Introductions and *być* | *jestem, jesteś, jest…*, greetings, *ty* vs *Pan/Pani* |
| 2 | Nouns and gender | Masculine / feminine / neuter from the ending, and the exceptions |
| 3 | Having and describing | *mieć*, adjective endings *-y/-a/-e* (and *-i/-ie* after k, g) |
| 4 | Present tense: -am, -asz verbs | *czytać, mieszkać, mieć* |
| 5 | Present tense: -ę, -isz / -esz verbs | *mówić, robić, pisać* |
| 6 | Negation and the genitive | *nie ma…*, *nie mam czasu*: negation changes the case |
| 7 | Accusative | What you see, want, buy |
| 8 | Numbers, time and prices | 0–1000, *ile kosztuje?*, telling time |
| 9 | Going places | *iść / jechać* (now) vs *chodzić / jeździć* (habit) |
| 10 | Instrumental | *jestem studentem*, *z kim?* (with whom) |
| 11 | Locative | *gdzie? w domu, w Polsce*, *o czym?* |
| 12 | Past tense | *byłem / byłam*: the past shows the speaker's gender |
| 13 | Aspect | Perfective vs imperfective pairs (*robić / zrobić*) |
| 14 | Future tense | *będę robić* vs *zrobię* |
| 15 | A2 milestone | A 10-minute conversation with the Tutor about your life, past and plans |

## Notes
- **Resources:** the coursebook *Polski, krok po kroku* (A1–A2) and the *Real Polish* podcast for
  easy listening.
- Cases look scary in a table. Learn them as **questions** instead (*kto? co?* → nominative,
  *kogo? czego?* → genitive…) and one at a time, as the roadmap does.
